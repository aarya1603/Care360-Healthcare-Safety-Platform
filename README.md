# 💙 Care360

> **Technology that cares.**

Care360 is a web-based healthcare and safety support platform designed to make essential healthcare information, emergency assistance, medicines, safety resources, games, and AI-powered guidance easily accessible from one place.

The platform focuses on **simplicity, accessibility, and quick access to important resources**, especially for users who may find complicated digital healthcare systems difficult to navigate.

---

## 🌟 Features

### 🤖 AI-Powered Care360 Assistant

Care360 includes an AI chatbot that helps users interact with the platform using natural language.

Users can ask questions and receive AI-generated responses through the integrated AI backend.

**Features:**
- Natural-language interaction
- AI-generated responses
- Simple chatbot interface
- Accessible from the main website
- Loading and error handling

---

### 🚨 Emergency Support

The Emergency section provides quick access to important emergency resources.

Users can:

- View emergency information
- Access emergency support
- Add trusted family members or caregivers
- Store emergency contact numbers
- Call saved emergency contacts directly
- Clear the complete emergency contact list

This allows users to keep important contacts available when they may be needed quickly.

---

### 💊 Medicines

The Medicines section provides an organized interface for accessing medicine-related information.

The platform is designed to make medicine information easier to find and navigate.

---

### 🛡️ Safety & Care

Care360 provides safety and care resources through a dedicated section.

The goal is to provide users with useful information in a simple and accessible format.

---

### 🎮 Games

Care360 includes simple games that can provide entertainment and engagement.

The platform includes browser-based games such as:

- Tic-Tac-Toe
- Snake
- Rock Paper Scissors
- Sudoku
- Guess the Number

These games are designed to run directly in the browser.

---

### 📩 Contact & Feedback

Users can contact the Care360 team through the Contact section.

The platform provides:

- Contact form
- Support email
- Feedback form
- Care360 rating system
- Customer reviews
- Feedback submission through email

Users can share their experience and suggestions to help improve Care360.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Healthcare information and emergency resources are often spread across multiple applications and websites.

Users may need to:

- Search for healthcare information
- Find emergency contacts
- Look for medicine-related information
- Find safety resources
- Contact support
- Navigate complicated interfaces

This can become especially difficult during stressful or urgent situations.

### Care360 aims to solve this by bringing these resources together into a single, simple platform.
